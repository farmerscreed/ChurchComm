Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

Welcome to Voxspiri Scheduler ("we," "our," or "us"), a service provided by LawOne Cloud LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our social media scheduling tool designed to post content to TikTok on your behalf.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our core scheduling service, we collect the following information when you authorize our application:

  • OAuth Access Tokens: Required to authenticate with TikTok on your behalf.
  • TikTok User ID: To identify your account and ensure content is posted to the correct profile.
  • Content Metadata: Information about the posts you schedule, such as captions, media links, and scheduled times.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely to provide the Voxspiri Scheduler service. Specifically, your data is used to:

  • Authenticate your account with TikTok.
  • Publish approved and scheduled content to your TikTok account on your behalf.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your privacy and the security of your accounts are our top priority:

  • All OAuth access tokens and related data are stored securely on our private servers using industry-standard encryption.
  • We never share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information or tokens to any third parties for marketing or any other purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Our service relies on the TikTok API to function. By using Voxspiri Scheduler, you acknowledge that your data interacts with TikTok's services. We comply with TikTok's Developer terms (developers.tiktok.com). For more information on how TikTok handles your data, please refer to their respective Privacy Policy.

5. Token Revocation and Data Deletion

You remain in full control of your account:

  • You can revoke Voxspiri Scheduler's access to your TikTok account at any time directly through your TikTok account settings.
  • Upon revocation, our application will immediately lose the ability to post on your behalf.
